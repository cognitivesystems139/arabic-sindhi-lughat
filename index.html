Arabic-Sindhi Dictionary

عربي سنڌي لغت

An Android app for looking up Arabic words with their Sindhi meanings. All dictionary features work offline, with no account.

The dictionary data is based on Arabi-Sindhi Lughat by Prof. Ghulam Hussain Jalbāni (2 volumes, 1988 and 1990), which in turn is based on al-Farāʾid al-Durriyyah, J. G. Hava's Arabic-English dictionary.
