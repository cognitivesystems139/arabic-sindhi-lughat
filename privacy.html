Privacy Policy

App: Arabic-Sindhi Dictionary (عربي سنڌي لغت), Android package com.cognitivesystems.arabicsindhi.
Developer: CognitiveSystems.
Last updated: 31 October 2026.

Summary

The app has no account and no sign-in. It does not collect, store or send what you search for or which entries you read. The dictionary works fully offline. The only network use is one small ad banner on the search screen.

What the app itself does with your data

  • Your searches and the entries you open stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.
  • The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • The app has no analytics and no crash-reporting service.
  • The app does not sell or share personal data, because it does not collect any.

Ads

The app shows one small banner ad, provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show the ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app connects to Google's servers. Google may collect and use information such as your IP address, device and software details (for example device model, operating system version, language), the app's identity, and diagnostic and performance data, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This information is handled by Google under its own policies, not by the developer.

  • The app asks for non-personalised ads only.
  • The app does not request the Android advertising ID: the permission for it is removed from the app.
  • No dictionary content, search text or usage data is given to the ad SDK.

More information: How Google uses data in ads, Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app requests only permissions needed by the ad banner:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to load the banner ad and check whether a network is available.
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE: added by the ad SDK for its background work.

A version of the app without ads would request no permissions at all.

Children and Google Play Families Policy

The app is a dictionary for a general audience, and its target age groups include children aged 9 to 13. This app is committed to following the Google Play Families Policy.

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and has no accounts, chat, social features or purchases. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
